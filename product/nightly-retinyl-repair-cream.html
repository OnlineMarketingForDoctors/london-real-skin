About this product

A topical 0.8% retinyl to combat acne and unblock pores whilst repairing sun-damaged skin. Increases cell renewal and collagen remodelling resulting in plumper, firmer, more evenly pigmented skin. Gentle enough to use every night with moisturising 1% hyaluronic acid.

Directions
