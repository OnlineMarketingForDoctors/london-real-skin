Why London Real Skin

Six reasons
patients stay

Dermatology-led, always

Every protocol sits under Medical Director Dr Martin Wade, on the GMC specialist register for dermatology and a Fellow of the Australasian College of Dermatologists.

CQC-registered facility

We treat inside a Care Quality Commission registered clinic, alongside The London Skin and Hair Clinic. The standards are medical, not cosmetic.

Complimentary consultations

Every treatment starts with an in-depth skin analysis at no cost, including the honest conversation about what won’t work for you.

Technology chosen on evidence

Sciton BBL & MOXI, BTL Exion, EmSculpt Neo and SkinXcell, selected from clinical studies rather than trade shows.

Personalised plans, real follow-up

Tailored protocols, post-care emailed the same day, and review appointments built into the plan rather than sold back to you.

Interest-free finance

0% finance over 3–12 months through GoCardless, with an upfront saving on courses. Terms and conditions apply.
