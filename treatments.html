Category 04

For men

Roughly a third of our patients are men. Treatment is tailored to address the concerns that matter most, from congestion and oil to pigmentation, texture and visible signs of ageing.

Beard growth changes what is possible. BBL for men is usually a half-face course, because treating over the beard disrupts hair growth.
