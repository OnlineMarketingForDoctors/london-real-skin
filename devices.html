Devices

The devices behind the results

Six platforms, chosen on the strength of the clinical evidence behind them rather than the marketing around them. Here is what each one is, and what it is honestly for.

Book a free consultation See the platforms

Worth saying first

A device is not
a treatment plan

Clinics like to lead with hardware because hardware photographs well. It is the least interesting part of the decision. The same laser can help one patient and set another back, and nothing on this page changes that.

Which platform is used, at what setting, and how many times, is decided at consultation after your skin has been assessed properly. This page is here so you know what we own and why, not so you can pick one.

The platforms

What we own,
and what it does

01 / Sciton

Sciton MOXI

A 1927nm fractional non-ablative laser. It works by treating a fraction of the skin at a time, which is what keeps the recovery short: bronzing and fine flaking for a few days rather than weeks indoors.

Technology
1927nm fractional non-ablative
Works on
Tone, texture, early pigment
Downtime
A few days of bronzing and flaking
Typical course
3 sessions
From
£1,500 for a course of 3

What we use it for

Sciton MOXI Fractional Resurfacing Laser Facial Rejuvenation MOXI for Men

Concerns it addresses

Pigmentation & Age Spots Fine Lines & Wrinkles Acne Scarring

02 / Sciton

Sciton BBL Hero

Broadband light, filtered to target either the red of a vessel or the brown of a pigment patch. Fast enough to cover the body as well as the face, which is why it is the workhorse of the room.

Technology
Filtered broadband light
Works on
Redness, sun damage, age spots
Downtime
Minimal; pigment darkens, then lifts
Typical course
3 sessions
From
£1,200 for a course of 3

What we use it for

Sciton BBL for Rosacea, Vascular Lesions and Pigmentation Sciton BBL for Pigmentation Dye VL Rosacea & Vascular IPL

Concerns it addresses

Rosacea Pigmentation & Age Spots Thread Veins

03 / BTL

EmSculpt Neo

Radiofrequency and high-intensity focused electromagnetic energy delivered at the same time, so the muscle contracts while the tissue above it is heated. Thirty minutes, lying down, four sessions in a typical course.

Technology
HIFEM with synchronised radiofrequency
Works on
Abdomen, buttocks, arms, calves
Session
30 minutes
Downtime
None; muscle soreness is normal
From
£199 per session

What we use it for

Emsculpt NEO Body Contouring with Skin Tightening London

04 / BTL

BTL Exion

Radiofrequency microneedling that measures the skin’s impedance as it goes and adjusts the energy to match, rather than running a fixed setting across a face that is not uniform.

Technology
RF microneedling, impedance monitored
Works on
Laxity, texture, collagen remodelling
Downtime
24 to 48 hours of redness
Typical course
3 to 4 sessions
From
£280

What we use it for

Fractional Radiofrequency Skin Tightening SkinXcell RF Micro-needling

Crepey Skin Acne Scarring Fine Lines & Wrinkles

A radiofrequency platform with three interchangeable handpieces, so the depth and the coverage change with the area being treated. The one we reach for on the neck and around the eye.

Works on

Downtime

£200

Also in the clinic

Four more, quietly useful

Not every piece of equipment needs a page of its own. These four do a specific job, often alongside one of the platforms above.

  • 07

    Dermalux Triwave

    Medical LED phototherapy across three wavelengths. Used on its own for inflammation and redness, and after resurfacing to settle the skin down.

    Dermalux LED Phototherapy London
  • 08

    Sterex

    Advanced cosmetic procedures by electrolysis. The precise route for skin tags, thread veins and small benign lesions, one at a time, under magnification.

    Skin Tags
  • 09

    HydraFacial

    Cleansing, exfoliation, extraction and hydration in one pass. The treatment people book before an event, and the one that pairs well with almost everything else.

    HydraFacial London
  • 10

    Kleresca

    Biophotonic light with a photoconverter gel, used for acne and for rejuvenation. Gentle enough for skin that will not tolerate a laser.

    Kleresca Light Therapy
