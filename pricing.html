How pricing works

No surprises,
and no pressure

  • 01

    The consultation is free

    An in-depth skin analysis before anything is booked. It costs nothing and commits you to nothing, and it is as likely to end in skincare as in a course of treatment.

  • 02

    Prices are per session

    Unless a line says otherwise. Where the evidence supports a course, we will tell you how many sessions before you commit to the first one.

  • 03

    Interest-free finance over 3 to 12 months

    Flexible terms to spread a course of treatment, with a saving for paying upfront. Terms and conditions apply.

  • 04

    You get the total in writing

    Your plan, the number of sessions and the full cost, confirmed before you book. The figures on this page are a guide; your quotation is the price.
