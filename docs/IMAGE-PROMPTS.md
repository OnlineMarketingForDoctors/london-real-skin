# London Real Skin — AI Image Prompt Sheet

Model: **Nano Banana Pro** (`nano_banana_pro`) via Higgsfield · Resolution **2K**

Every prompt below is assembled from three parts: a **scene**, plus two reusable blocks that
keep the whole page visually consistent — a palette lock and a skin-realism lock.

---

## Block A — Palette lock (appended to every prompt)

> Colour grade locked to a strict brand palette: deep desaturated navy-blue **#2A4664** in the
> shadows and mid-tones, a single teal-cyan **#1DB0BA** accent appearing only in equipment glow,
> light refraction or a small styling detail, and pale cool off-white **#E7EEF5** in walls,
> linens, garments and highlights. Cool colour temperature, low saturation, high-end editorial
> grade. Absolutely no warm orange, gold, amber, beige, magenta or purple tones. Clean, calm,
> expensive, restrained.

## Block B — Skin realism lock (appended to every prompt containing a face or skin)

> Hyper-realistic human skin, photographed not rendered. Clearly visible pores across the nose,
> cheeks and forehead. Fine vellus hair catching the light along the jaw and hairline. Natural
> textural unevenness — a small cluster of freckles, faint under-eye shadow, slight natural
> redness at the nostril crease and inner cheek, a few fine lines at the outer corner of the eye,
> one or two small blemishes or a healing spot. Natural sebum sheen on the T-zone rather than
> uniform matte. Real subsurface scattering, true-to-life skin translucency at the ears and
> nostrils. The skin must NOT look airbrushed, waxy, plastic, smoothed, poreless, filtered or
> AI-perfect — no beauty retouching whatsoever. Shot on a full-frame camera, 85mm lens at f/2.0,
> soft north-facing window light. Natural, unglamorised, believable human being.

## Images deliberately NOT generated

| Slot | Why |
|---|---|
| **Patient before & after photos** | Generating synthetic "patient results" for a CQC-regulated medical clinic would be fabricated medical advertising, and would breach ASA/CAP rules on before-and-after imagery. The section is built and styled with empty, clearly-labelled frames awaiting the client's real consented photographs. |
| **Team headshots** | The team are real, named, GMC-registered clinicians. Generating synthetic faces and captioning them "Dr Martin Wade" would misrepresent identifiable people. Built as styled monogram placeholders pending the real files. |
| **Review screenshots** | The review *text* is real and used verbatim. The Google screenshot images are not needed — the carousel renders the reviews as native cards. |
