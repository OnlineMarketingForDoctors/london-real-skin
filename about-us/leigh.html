Our team

Leigh

Aesthetician

Leigh brings a wealth of experience from many years working in the aesthetics industry, with particular expertise in light- and laser-based treatments. She has developed an advanced understanding of how to use these technologies safely and effectively to deliver outstanding results, whether addressing skin rejuvenation, pigmentation, or other concerns.

Known for her warm and bubbly personality, Leigh creates a welcoming and supportive environment for every client. She takes the time to understand each person’s unique skin at the time of treatment, carefully tailoring her approach to ensure the best possible outcome. Her clients value not only her professional expertise but also the genuine care and attention she provides throughout their journey.
