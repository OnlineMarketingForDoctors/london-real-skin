Three devices, one plan

The right technology for your skin

We choose between three premium platforms, or combine them, depending on whether your skin needs collagen induction, deeper radiofrequency remodelling, targeted ultrasound or a needle-free option.

Medical microneedling

xCellaris Pro Twist™

A premium medical microneedling device that creates precise, controlled micro-channels in the skin. This triggers a natural wound-healing response, stimulating collagen and elastin while driving regenerative serums deeper for better results.

  • Precise, adjustable needle depth
  • Collagen and elastin induction
  • Enhanced serum delivery
  • Consistent, comfortable treatment
RF microneedling + fractional RF

Gold-Standard RF

Two treatments in one. Advanced microneedling combined with Gold-Standard 2MHz radiofrequency and post-treatment cryocooling, plus a needle-free Fractional RF option for sensitive or needle-phobic patients. We use it most for general skin rejuvenation and for lifting and tightening larger body areas.

  • Gold-Standard 2MHz radiofrequency
  • Ideal for general rejuvenation
  • Great for larger body treatments
  • Needle-free Fractional RF option
RF microneedling + ultrasound

BTL Exion

The latest generation of radiofrequency microneedling, pairing AI-controlled fractional RF with targeted ultrasound. That added ultrasound reaches deeper to remodel the skin, which makes Exion our first choice for acne scarring, while also boosting the skin's own hyaluronic acid and collagen.

  • AI-controlled RF microneedling
  • Targeted ultrasound energy
  • Our first choice for acne scarring
  • Boosts natural hyaluronic acid

The device is only half the story

Regenerative serums,
chosen for your skin

Microneedling opens a window for active ingredients to work at a deeper level. That is why we personalise every treatment, selecting the regenerative serum, or combination, that best suits your skin.

RevitalisationNCTF®

A revitalisation complex of non-crosslinked hyaluronic acid with over 50 active ingredients, vitamins, amino acids and antioxidants. Ideal for dehydrated, dull or early-ageing skin.

RepairPolynucleotides

Highly purified DNA fragments that encourage tissue regeneration and calm inflammation. Excellent for delicate, thin or acne-scarred skin and eye rejuvenation.

Exosome technologyPurasomes®

Advanced exosome formulations by DermaFocus that support cellular communication, boost collagen and speed recovery for firmer, more resilient skin with minimal downtime.

Growth factorsCalecim® Professional

A proprietary blend of growth factors, cytokines and signalling proteins that support tissue repair, elasticity and gradual improvements in skin strength and texture.

Growth factorsAQ Skin Solutions®

Human-derived growth factors that stimulate the skin's own regenerative pathways, ideal for ageing skin, reduced collagen, fine lines and overall rejuvenation.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Microneedling with the xCellaris Pro Twist creates fine micro-channels to stimulate collagen and elastin. RF microneedling adds Gold-Standard 2MHz radiofrequency energy at depth, for stronger tightening and remodelling. At your consultation we recommend the right approach, and sometimes a combination, for your skin.

Fine lines and wrinkles, sagging or crepey skin, dehydration, uneven tone and texture, acne and trauma scars, stretch marks, enlarged pores and skin laxity, on both the face and the body.

A topical anaesthetic can be applied before microneedling, and multi-depth control lets us tailor the treatment to your sensitivity. The cryocooling tool soothes the skin after the RF treatment to help manage any warmth or redness.

This depends on your concern, but most patients have between three and six treatments for optimal results. Your practitioner will give you a clear, realistic plan at your consultation.

Some patients notice a difference within two to three weeks, with results building as collagen regenerates over your course. Downtime is minimal, usually mild redness for one to four hours. Maintenance treatments are advised every six to twelve months.

The device is only half the story. Microneedling opens a window for active ingredients to work deeper, so we personalise every treatment with a regenerative serum such as NCTF, polynucleotides, exosomes or growth factors, chosen for your skin, to get the best possible result.
