Conditions

Tell us what is bothering you

Most people arrive with a concern rather than a treatment in mind. These are the ones we see most often, what each one actually is, and what we use for it.

Book a free consultation See what we treat

How this works

Concern first,
machine second

A laser is not a diagnosis. Two people with what looks like the same brown patch can need two completely different plans, and one of them can be made worse by the treatment that helps the other.

So every plan here starts with a proper look at the skin, under magnification, by someone qualified to say what they are seeing. That consultation is free, and it is as likely to end in skincare and a review as it is in a course of treatment.

What we treat

Skin concerns,
seen properly

Each one links through to the treatments we use for it. If what is bothering you is not listed, it is still worth asking.

Acne Scarring

The textural changes left behind once acne has settled; such as rolling, boxcar or ice-pick scars. The type of scarring responds differently to treatment, which is why we assess your skin first to determine the most appropriate approach and modalities for you.

What we use

Laser Skin Resurfacing RF Microneedling London Microneedling London MOXI Laser London Medical-Grade Skin Peels London
About acne Scarring

Pigmentation & Age Spots

Pigmentation such as sun damage, melasma and post-inflammatory pigmentation can look similar, but they behave very differently and require different approaches. Accurate assessment is the first step to choosing the right treatment for your skin.

What we use

Microneedling London Sciton BBL London MOXI Laser London Medical-Grade Skin Peels London
About pigmentation & Age Spots

Rosacea

Persistent redness, flushing and visible blood vessels, often with identifiable triggers. While rosacea cannot be cured, it can be effectively managed with the right approach to reduce its appearance and keep flare-ups under control.

What we use

Sciton BBL London Dye-VL Laser London Kleresca Light Therapy London
About rosacea

Thread Veins

Fine, visible blood vessels or broken capillaries that commonly appear on the face. We use light-based devices with specific wavelengths to selectively target the affected vessels while minimising impact on the surrounding skin.

What we use

Dye-VL Laser London Sciton BBL London
About thread Veins

Skin Tags

Small, benign skin growths that commonly develop where skin rubs against skin or clothing. They can usually be removed quickly and safely, but it’s important to have them assessed before treatment rather than picking or removing them yourself.

What we use

Skin Tags
About skin Tags

Genital Skin Tags & Warts

Benign lesions in an area people put off asking about. Assessed and removed discreetly, in a CQC-registered facility, by clinicians who see them every week.

What we use

Skin Tags
About genital Skin Tags & Warts

Teeth Grinding

Bruxism, and the widening of the jaw that comes with it. Relaxing the masseter eases the clenching and softens the jawline at the same time.

What we use

Anti-Wrinkle Injections London
About teeth Grinding

Fine Lines & Wrinkles

Fine lines and wrinkles can develop from facial expression, sun exposure, or a combination of both. Understanding what is causing the line helps us determine whether the most effective approach is to relax the movement, resurface the skin, or combine treatments.

What we use

Anti-Wrinkle Injections London Dermal Fillers London MOXI Laser London RF Microneedling London Microneedling London
About fine Lines & Wrinkles

Ageing Skin

Changes in volume, skin laxity and dullness often develop together as the skin ages. Addressing these concerns effectively usually requires a personalised combination approach, using different modalities to target different aspects of ageing over several months rather than relying on a single treatment or appointment.

What we use

Profhilo London Polynucleotides London Dermal Fillers London RF Skin Tightening London Laser Skin Rejuvenation London
About ageing Skin

Crepey Skin

Fine, delicate, papery-looking skin that can develop on areas such as the neck, chest, upper arms and under the eyes. Unlike traditional wrinkles, crepey skin is primarily a change in skin texture and laxity, requiring a different treatment approach.

What we use

RF Skin Tightening London RF Microneedling London Microneedling London Body Contouring London Profhilo London
About crepey Skin

Stretch Marks & Cellulite

Both stretch marks and cellulite reflect structural changes within the skin rather than simply surface imperfections. Treatment can improve their appearance, but complete removal is not realistic. We’ll always be clear about what treatment can achieve before you decide to proceed.

What we use

Microneedling London Stretch Mark Treatment London RF Skin Tightening London Laser Scar Revision London
About stretch Marks & Cellulite

Excess Hair

Unwanted hair on the face or body can vary significantly in colour, thickness and growth pattern. We use IPL and BBL light-based devices, using specific wavelengths to target the hair follicle effectively while taking your skin type and hair characteristics into account.

What we use

Laser Hair Removal London
About excess Hair

When it is not cosmetic
